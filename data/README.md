# Dataset Information

## Data Source 
Data was found on [Kaggle.com](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/shivamb/netflix-shows) from user "shivamb"

## Data
- `netflix_titles.csv`: dataset containing 8000+ observations recording various pieces of information about Netflix titles, such as the date the title was added, the director(s), the cast, and the rating of the title.

## Codebook
- `show_id`: unique ID for every movie/TV show
- `type`: identifies each observation as a movie or a TV show
- `title`: title of the movie/TV show
- `director`: director(s) of the movie/TV show
- `cast`: cast members of the movie/TV show
- `country`: country/countries where the movie/TV show was produced
- `date_added`: date the title was added to the Netflix catalog
- `release_year:` year the title was released
- `rating`: the rating of the title using the Motion Picture Association (MPA) rating guidelines
- `duration`: the length of the title (minutes for movies, seasons for shows)
- `listed_in`: genre of the title
- `description`: summary description of the title
